Scoundrel
A Roguelike Card Game · Gage & Bieg
How to Play

The Dungeon

Start with 20 HP. Each turn, 4 cards form a Room. You must face 3 of them — the 4th carries into the next room.

You may avoid a room, sending all 4 cards to the bottom of the deck. You cannot avoid two rooms in a row.

Card Types

  • Monster Clubs & Spades — value = damage dealt to you.
  • Weapon Diamonds — must equip on pickup, replacing old weapon.
  • Potion Hearts — restore HP (max 20). Only one per turn.

J=11 · Q=12 · K=13 · A=14

Combat

Barehanded: Take the monster's full value as damage.

With weapon: Damage = max(0, monster − weapon). Monster is placed on the weapon.

Weapon degrades: after use, it can only fight monsters of strictly lower value than the last monster it slew. The weapon isn't lost — just limited.

Scoring

Victory — clear the deck. Score = remaining HP. (Bonus: if HP = 20 and last card was a potion, score = 20 + potion value.)

Defeat — HP hits 0. Score = negative sum of all remaining monsters.
